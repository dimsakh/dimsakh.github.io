Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Invoice Automations ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner for personal business use. It is used only with the owner's own Google account.

Data the app accesses

With the user's permission granted through Google OAuth, the app reads, creates and updates files in the user's Google Drive in order to process invoices and save the results.

How data is used

Data from Google Drive is used solely to perform the invoice processing requested by the user. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage and security

Files remain in the user's Google Drive. OAuth tokens are stored only in the environment where the app runs and are used only to access the user's own Drive.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
